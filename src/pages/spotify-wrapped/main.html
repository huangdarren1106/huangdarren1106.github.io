How to find Spotify Wrapped in the app:

  1. Open the Spotify mobile app (iOS or Android)
  2. Look for the Wrapped banner on the home screen Or search for “Wrapped” in the Search tab
  3. Tap the story cards to view your stats.

When does Spotify stop counting your listening history?

Spotify doesn’t publish an exact cutoff date every year, but historically:

  • Listening usually stops being counted sometime in October or early November
  • Anything you play after that likely won’t affect that year’s Wrapped
  • Spotify uses the remaining weeks to prepare the data and stories.

This is why playing more songs and music genres in November usually don’t affect your Wrapped results.

How long does Spotify Wrapped stay available?

  • The Wrapped stories usually stay available until the end of the year or early January.
  • The “Your Top Songs” for the year usually stay in your library permanently
  • Once Wrapped is removed from the app, you won’t be able to rewatch the stories inside the Spotify app.

Why isn’t Spotify Wrapped showing up for me?

If you don’t see your Wrapped:

  • Make sure your Spotify mobile app is updated
  • Try logging out and back in
  • Search for “Wrapped” in the Search tab
  • Wait a bit as rollout can be staggered by region

For new or very inactive users, Spotify may not generate a full Wrapped for your accounts.

Can I see my old Spotify Wrapped?

  • Once Spotify Wrapped is removed from the app, you can’t rewatch old Wrapped stories in the app
  • Your Top Songs playlists from past years often remain in your library
  • Some users save screenshots or screen recordings of their Wrapped stories
  • You can use Spotify Wrapped alternatives to see your current Spotify statistics.

Alternatives when Spotify Wrapped is not available

When Spotify Wrapped is no longer available in the app, you can still use these tools to explore your listening habits and stats:

Spotify Stats (spotifystats.com)

Spotify Pie (huangdarren1106.github.io)

My Top Spotify (mytopspotify.io)

Music Pie Chart (musicpiechart.com)

These services connect to your account using Spotify’s official API and let you review your listening stats - top artists, songs, and genres - across different time ranges - from the last 4 weeks up to the past 12 months.
